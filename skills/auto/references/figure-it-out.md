2. Route the settled plan once more through the table in `auto`.
3. When a route fits, take its playbook from the first step the settled plan has not done.
4. When none fits, take the feature playbook from its second step, with the settled plan.

Done when: the settled plan runs under the playbook it fits, or the feature playbook has built and landed it.
